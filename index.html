Assumption register and model scope

All inputs are recorded below and in scenario JSON. One sol and one model year are editable time conventions. Unit conversions, mathematical constants, and derived quantities are not independent assumptions.

AssumptionCurrent valueUse in model

The synthetic daylight curve and two seasonal harmonics are screening approximations. Load shape is repeated each sol and normalized to scheduled energy. Battery thermal behavior, aging within the simulated year, grid faults, component outage chronology, fuel replenishment, and probabilistic weather sampling are outside this model. Changing the battery chemistry label does not supply new performance data; set the battery parameters for the selected technology.
